Navkis College of Engineering, Hassan (NCEH) is a state-of-the-art technical institution that provides an ideal atmosphere to the students to grow into world-class engineers. It was started in the year 2009-10 by Yagachi Education and Research Trust and is under the patronage of Navkis Group of Institutions, Bengaluru, from the year 2019-20. The College is located in a peaceful picturesque landscape providing a good academic ambience, supported by premium infrastructure and facilities necessary for imparting quality engineering education.

ABOUT US

At Navkis, we offer courses in various fields such as AIML, AIDS, CSE, ECE, Civil and MCA and we are proud to bring Design Thinking for Engineers for the first time ever. Our Design Labs, Innovation Labs, and Entrepreneurship cell provide our students with the opportunity to explore their creativity and develop innovative solutions to real-world problems.

Navkis College of Engineering, Hassan - an initiative of Sri M S Ramaiah, where we believe in nurturing talent and providing industry demanded courses for our students. Our campus is spread over 12 acres of land and is equipped with top-class amenities to provide our students with the best possible learning experience.

At Navkis, we offer courses in various fields such as AIML, AIDS, CSE, ECE, and Civil, and we are proud to bring Design Thinking for Engineers for the first time ever. Our Design Labs, Innovation Labs, and Entrepreneurship cell provide our students with the opportunity to explore their creativity and develop innovative solutions to real-world problems.

We understand the importance of research and learning beyond the classroom, which is why our campus is equipped with a library, wifi-enabled campus, cafeteria, transportation, and safe hostel facilities. We also provide monitored industry internships to our students to help them gain practical experience and exposure to the industry.

At Navkis, we believe that placement assistance is a critical component of our students' success, and we provide 100% placement assistance to our students. We have over 250+ corporate partners who work with us to ensure that our students have access to the best job opportunities in the industry.

We are proud of our students' achievements and are committed to providing them with the best possible education and opportunities to succeed. Join us at Navkis College of Engineering and be a part of a community that believes in nurturing talent and creating future leaders.
