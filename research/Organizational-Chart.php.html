Organizational Structure

Navkis College of Engineering, Hassan (NCEH) follows a well-defined and transparent organizational structure that ensures effective governance, academic leadership, administrative efficiency, and quality assurance. The structure clearly defines roles, responsibilities, reporting relationships, and decision-making authority, enabling smooth institutional functioning.

The organizational framework is designed to support academic excellence, student-centric services, statutory compliance, and continuous quality improvement, in alignment with AICTE, VTU, and UGC guidelines.

Governing & Strategic Leadership

At the apex of the organizational structure is the Management and Governing Council, which provides strategic direction, policy oversight, and long-term vision for the institution. The Governing Council ensures that institutional goals are aligned with regulatory requirements, academic standards, and societal needs.

Academic & Administrative Leadership

The Principal serves as the chief academic and administrative head of the institution and is responsible for implementing the policies and decisions of the Governing Council. The Principal is supported by academic leaders, administrative heads, and functional coordinators to ensure efficient institutional operations.

Academic Administration

Academic activities are coordinated through:

  • Heads of Departments (HoDs)
  • Program Coordinators and Faculty Members
  • First-Year and Program-Level Academic Committees

This structure ensures effective curriculum delivery, mentoring, assessment, research activities, and student development across all programs.

Quality Assurance & Institutional Cells

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) functions as the central mechanism for monitoring, reviewing, and enhancing academic and administrative quality. IQAC coordinates with various statutory and non-statutory committees, cells, and departments to ensure continuous quality improvement (CQI).

Supporting cells and committees include:

  • Examination & Evaluation
  • Research & Development
  • Training & Placement
  • Mentoring & Counselling
  • Student Welfare & Grievance Redressal
  • Women Cell, Anti-Ragging, and other statutory committees

Administrative & Support Services

Administrative and support functions are handled through clearly identified units such as:

  • Office Administration
  • Finance & Accounts
  • Human Resources
  • IT & Digital Systems
  • Library
  • Hostel Administration
  • Transport, Security, and Maintenance

These units work in coordination to provide seamless support to academic and student-centric activities.

Student-Centric Governance

The organizational structure promotes student participation and support through mentoring systems, student councils, clubs, professional bodies, and outreach units. This inclusive framework ensures holistic development, leadership opportunities, and a supportive campus environment.

Transparency & Accountability

The organizational structure of NCEH promotes:

  • Clear reporting mechanisms
  • Defined roles and responsibilities
  • Documentation and review processes
  • Periodic audits and evaluations

This enables transparency, accountability, and institutional resilience.
