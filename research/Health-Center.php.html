Health Centre

Navkis College of Engineering, Hassan is committed to ensuring the health, safety, and well-being of students, faculty, and staff through a well-established on-campus Health Centre. The institution recognizes that a healthy campus environment is essential for effective learning, productivity, and overall well-being.

The Health Centre functions as the first point of medical support within the campus and is equipped to handle routine health needs, minor illnesses, and emergency first aid.

Medical Facilities & Staffing

Full-Time Nursing Support

The Health Centre is staffed with a dedicated full-time qualified nurse who is available on campus during working hours to:

  • Provide immediate first aid and basic medical care
  • Attend to minor injuries, cuts, burns, sprains, and fever
  • Monitor students who feel unwell during academic hours
  • Maintain health records and first-aid inventory
Doctor on Call

The institution has an arrangement with a qualified medical doctor on call, ensuring:

  • Medical consultation whenever required
  • Timely medical advice for cases needing expert attention
  • Support during medical emergencies until further referral
Tie-Up with Multi-Speciality Hospital

Navkis College of Engineering has a formal Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with a nearby multi-speciality hospital to provide advanced medical support beyond campus facilities.

This collaboration ensures:

  • Priority medical consultation and treatment
  • Emergency care and hospitalization if required
  • Access to specialist doctors and diagnostic facilities
  • Seamless referral during medical emergencies

Services Offered

First Aid & Emergency Care
  • Immediate response to injuries and sudden illness
  • Stabilization of patients before referral
Preliminary Medication
  • Basic medicines for common ailments such as fever, cold, headache, stomach discomfort, and minor infections
  • Administration of prescribed medication under supervision
Health Monitoring & Support
  • Blood pressure and basic health checks
  • Observation and rest facilities for students feeling unwell
Emergency Coordination
  • Rapid communication with the doctor on call
  • Immediate referral to the partnered hospital when necessary

Student Safety & Well-Being

The Health Centre plays a vital role in:

  • Ensuring student safety during academic hours, laboratory sessions, sports, and events
  • Supporting hostel residents during medical needs
  • Promoting a safe and caring campus environment

Special attention is given to female students, hostel residents, and students with medical conditions, ensuring timely and confidential care.

Health Awareness & Preventive Care

In addition to routine medical support, the institution promotes preventive healthcare and awareness through:

  • Health awareness programs and camps
  • Guidance on hygiene, nutrition, and wellness
  • Coordination with institutional outreach and NSS activities for health initiatives

Institutional Commitment

Navkis College of Engineering, Hassan remains committed to providing a safe, healthy, and supportive campus. Through its on-campus Health Centre, full-time nursing support, doctor-on-call facility, and hospital tie-up, the institution ensures that medical care is accessible, timely, and reliable for all stakeholders.
